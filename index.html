Our purpose

Our goal is to empower the arts in our community.

Smaller, organized rooms for artists and designers.

  1. 01

    Artist support

    Space, time and visibility for people making work, plus promotion that puts their art in front of the community.

  2. 02

    Community gatherings

    Hands-on workshops and mixers for artists, makers and neighbors to meet, learn and trade ideas.

  3. 03

    Public programs

    Exhibitions, artist talks and performances that open the door to the street.
