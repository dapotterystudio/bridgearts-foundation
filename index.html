The purpose we’re building toward

Make room
for artists.

Bring art into everyday life.

Artists need places to work, and communities need places to encounter art. Bridge Arts Foundation is taking shape around both.

Our proposed focus connects artist support, shared learning, and public programs in Lambertville, alongside the creative community at 67 Bridge Street.

  1. 01

    Artist support

    Space, time and visibility for people making work.

  2. 02

    Shared learning

    Workshops where neighbors and artists learn side by side.

  3. 03

    Public programs

    Exhibitions and gatherings that open the door to the street.
